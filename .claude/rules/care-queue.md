# Care queue (product ADR-0054, product ADR-0056, product ADR-0069, product ADR-0071)

User-facing name **Care**; internal identifiers keep "Today".

## Aggregation (`TodayQueueAggregator`, pure)
- **The only source of Care tasks.** `TodayCareRepository.observeQueue()` combines the live repositories, settings and `dayChangeTicker()` and delegates to it. UI code never derives due tasks.
- **Inputs and horizon:** visible plants (`Plant.isVisible`: neither archived nor hidden, #915), care logs, custom reminders, issues, photos, settings and an explicit `LocalDate`. It must reuse `CareSchedule.computeStatus()`, never reimplement due rules. Tasks are ordered by due instant, then lowercase plant name, then task id.
- **Horizon (#910, #931):** `TodayQueueInput.horizonEnd: LocalDate` (default `defaultHorizonEnd(today)` = today + 1, `DEFAULT_UPCOMING_HORIZON_DAYS`) bounds Upcoming: a task due in `(today, horizonEnd]` is `Upcoming(epochDay)`, later ones are dropped, and `horizonEnd <= today` leaves only Overdue and Today. **Per kind (product ADR-0069):** `TodayQueueInput.nonWateringHorizonEnd: LocalDate?` (default `null` = share `horizonEnd`, read through `effectiveNonWateringHorizonEnd`, so `copy(horizonEnd = …)` and direct constructions never clip a widened window) bounds FERTILIZE, REPOT, CUSTOM_REMINDER, ISSUE_TREATMENT and PHOTO; WATER and the combined WATER_AND_FERTILIZE use `horizonEnd`. Only `TodayCareRepository.observeQueue()` with no caller horizon (the default path, `TodayViewModel`) sets `nonWateringHorizonEnd = today`, so Care shows Overdue / Today / Tomorrow for watering and only Overdue / Today for everything else; a repot plan whose season starts tomorrow is not on Care until the day. Each task stays its next occurrence only (no projected recurrences). The liquid-fertilizer combined rule, dormancy and away handling hold over any horizon. `observeQueue(horizonEnd: Flow<LocalDate>)` widens every kind to that date as one more input of the existing settings `combine()`, so there are no extra Room queries and still one `dayChangeTicker()`. The caller's flow is not rolled at midnight: a rolling horizon must re-emit its own date. Care's own screen never widens it.
- **Away mode (#897, product ADR-0065):** `TodayQueueInput.awayPeriods` (default empty) reaches `computeStatus(awayPeriods = …)` and sets `WateringTaskAction.isAwaySpanning`. `TodayCareRepository` takes an optional `AwayPeriodRepository` and folds `observeAll()` into its settings `combine()` (now 5 inputs with the horizon, the typed-overload maximum; the next one needs a wrapper `combine`). Trips change no due date, bucket or task: Care keeps showing tasks during a trip.
- **Care banner (#898):** `TodayScreen` takes an optional `awayModeViewModel` and renders `AwayModeBanner` in a `Column` **above** the grid/empty states (the grid sits in a `weight(1f)` `Box`). It shows while a trip is active and for `AWAY_BANNER_LEAD_DAYS` (7) days before one starts, with **I'm back** while away; messages (removed sitter visits) go to Care's snackbar host. The banner is the top element, so when #850's week strip lands, put the banner above it (coordinate there). Details: `rules/away-mode.md`. Slice 4 (#899) adds **Plan a sitter visit** (while away) and **Before you leave** as banner actions, passed in as `AwayModeNavigation`. **Before you leave** shows for the whole lead-in (`AwayModeUiState.canOpenBeforeYouLeave`: from `AWAY_BANNER_LEAD_DAYS` days before the trip through the last day at home, #969, product ADR-0076), not only the last day, and never while away; the sitter flow reads due dates only through `TodayQueueAggregator` with a widened `horizonEnd`, never through this screen. Slice 5 (#900) adds a **sitter visits to confirm** prompt under the banner (same `Column`, after the trip; `TodayScreen` takes an optional `confirmVisitViewModel` and the banner hosts the sheet); it is not a queue task.
- **Liquid-fertilizer plants** get one combined water-and-fertilize task only when watering falls in `horizonEnd` and the fertilizing task exists (it is built under the narrower non-watering window, so on Care's default path fertilizing must itself be Overdue or Today) and is due no later than watering. The combined task sits in the *watering* bucket (Watering · Tomorrow if watering is tomorrow). They never get a standalone fertilizer task. Accepted gaps: fertilizing due tomorrow with watering tomorrow shows a plain watering tile (fertilizing waits for the next watering or Plant Detail), and fertilizing overdue with watering 2+ days out shows no task.
- **Issues and photos:** an active issue relabels only its linked custom-reminder task. Photo tasks use the newest care-log or gallery photo and ignore the session-only reminder-popup suppression.
- **Repot plans** are bucketed by season state (season start while upcoming, Today while in season, Overdue on the season's last day once ended), never by the raw past start date.
- **Watered today (#939, product ADR-0071):** `TodayQueueSnapshot.wateredToday: List<TodayWateredPlant(plant, wateredAt)>` is built in `build()` from the logs it already groups by plant: visible plants only, one entry per plant whose newest WATER `loggedAt` falls in `[input.today, today + 1)` (range check on `toStartOfDayMillis()`, never `LocalDate.now()`), ordered newest first, then lowercase name, then plant id. Only WATER counts (a combined action through its paired WATER log); every other type is ignored. It is independent of `horizonEnd`/`nonWateringHorizonEnd`, so the sitter flow and other callers ignore it, and it needs no repository change (the existing `dayChangeTicker()` empties it at midnight). `TodayQueueSnapshot.hasTasksDueNow` is true when any task is not `Upcoming`; the UI uses it and does no date math. The snapshot defaults `wateredToday` to empty so hand-built snapshots keep working.
- **Sections:** `careTypeSections()` (`domain/today/TodayCareTask.kt`) returns a flat list ordered Watering · Overdue → Watering · Today → Watering · Tomorrow → Issue treatments → Fertilizing → Custom reminders → Repotting → Photos, empty ones hidden, queue order kept inside each. Watering (incl. the combined task) yields up to three `TodayCareTypeSection` entries with `wateringGroup` = `TodayWateringGroup` OVERDUE / TODAY / TOMORROW, mapped 1:1 from `TodayTaskBucket` (any `Upcoming` is Tomorrow, safe because Care never widens the horizon), so the UI does no date math; every other entry has `wateringGroup == null`. Every entry carries a distinct-plant `plantCount`.

## Screen (`TodayCareGrid.kt`)
- **Layout:** a `LazyVerticalGrid` of square plant tiles, 2–4 per row (capped `CareGridCells`).
- **Four screen states (`TodayScreen`, product ADR-0071):** (1) `activePlantCount == 0` first use; (2) `tasks` and `wateredToday` both empty: `TodayCaughtUpState`; (3) no tasks but `wateredToday` non-empty: `TodayCareGrid` with only the Watered today section, then `TodayCaughtUpContent` (icon + `today_caught_up`) as a full-span item below it; (4) otherwise `TodayCareGrid`. `TodayCareGrid` also takes `wateredToday`, `hasTasksDueNow`, the expansion Boolean and its toggle, all defaulted so older call sites compile.
- **Watered today section (`WateredTodayRow.kt`):** placed by `wateredTodayInsertIndex(sections)`, right after the last Watering · Overdue/Today section (before the first section that is not one, first when none, last when only those). Header is a `stickyHeader(key = "section:WATERED_TODAY")` using the shared `CareGroupHeader` and `onToggleSection`, so title (`Watered today · N`), `heading()`, Expanded/Collapsed and collapse-then-scroll all match; the key lives in the same `collapsedGroups` set. Below it one full-span item holds a `FlowRow` of fixed slots (not emitted while collapsed): up to `WATERED_TODAY_VISIBLE_COUNT` (5) round photos with a decorative check badge and the name, then a "+N" circle (`care_watered_today_more_cd` plural) that expands to every plant plus a "Show less" circle. Slot width is `wateredTodaySlotWidthPx(constraints.maxWidth, density)`: floor division of the row's pixel width by 6, capped at 72dp and never below 48dp, then converted back to `Dp` (a `Dp` width rounds each slot to the nearest pixel, so six slots can total more than the row and wrap the sixth); the row is left-aligned. The expansion Boolean is a separate `rememberSaveable` in `TodayScreen` (survives tab switches and rotation, resets on restart, not reset at midnight, inert at 5 or fewer). Each photo is one merged `Role.Button` named `"<name>, watered today"` (the photo and badge are `clearAndSetSemantics`), tap calls `actions.onOpen(plantId, PlantDetailTab.WATER)`, and there is **no long-press, `onLongClick` or `customActions`** (a watered entry is not a task; "Water" would hit the same-day duplicate guard). The badge uses the primary colour, never due-status colours or `IssuePurple`.
- **"Nothing due today" line:** when `tasks` is non-empty, `wateredToday` is empty and `!hasTasksDueNow` (only Tomorrow tasks), the grid's first item is a full-span, non-sticky `NothingDueTodayLine` (decorative check icon + `care_nothing_due_today`, no heading, no click action).
- **Headers:** every section is a top-level, same-style header read `title · N` (N = distinct plants): `Watering · Overdue · N`, `Watering · Today · N`, `Watering · Tomorrow · N`, `Fertilizing · N`, … There is no parent "Watering" header. Collapse on tap; expose `heading()`, `stateDescription` (Expanded/Collapsed) and an Expand/Collapse click label. Collapse keys: `watering:OVERDUE|TODAY|TOMORROW` for the watering entries (`TodayCareTypeSection.key`), `section:<NAME>` for the rest. The collapsed set is `rememberSaveable` in `TodayScreen`, so it survives tab switches and rotation but resets on restart: a forgotten collapse must never hide the next day's care. Stale keys from older builds are inert.
- **Sticky headers (#931):** each header is a native `LazyGridScope.stickyHeader(key, contentType) { index -> }` (full-line span), so the pinned header is the same item (one heading in semantics) and pins at the top of the grid's `weight(1f)` Box, below the Away banner. It has an opaque `colorScheme.background` and the grid's `contentPadding.top` is 0 so tiles never show above it. Collapsing a header that is at or above the viewport top (`gridState.firstVisibleItemIndex >= index`) calls `gridState.scrollToItem(index)` so the collapsed header is the first visible item; a header already on screen collapses without scrolling.
- **Tiles:** no due-date text and no overdue styling (#842). The content description always names the plant and the task. A REPOT plan tile's second line is "Planned for spring".
- **Tap:** opens Plant Detail on the matching tab via the `tab` route arg (`TodayCareKind.plantDetailTab()`; combined → Fertilize; #843).
- **Long-press:** opens a `DropdownMenu` of that kind's actions only (`careMenuActions()` in `CareMenuAction.kt`), mirrored as semantics `customActions` + `onLongClickLabel`. Each entry calls the existing `TodayViewModel` handler/prompt. Never add inline buttons back or reimplement a prompt. **Water** and **Water & fertilize** (#838, product ADR-0067) open the shared "Log watering" date picker (`LogWateringDatePickerDialog`, with the optional "Soil was already too dry" tick) instead of logging at once; confirming calls `QuickLogUseCase.wateringGateFacts()` and the shared `evaluateWateringGate()` from `WateringReasonGate.kt`, then `TodayViewModel.completeWater`/`completeFertilizing(taskId, reason, loggedAt)` or the reason sheet. The picker task id, the reason sheet's task id, its `loggedAt` and its gap direction are `rememberSaveable` in `TodayScreen`. Never reimplement the gate here.
- **No bulk completion or selection on Care** (#842): the bottom bar never hides for Care (`shouldShowBottomNavigation()` takes only Plant List's selection flag), and there is no plant-grouped layout or flag. `completeCustomReminder()` (single Mark done) and Plant List's `bulkLog()` remain.
- **Reminder photos:** Care, Plant List and Calendar all save them through the one transactional `QuickLogUseCase.saveReminderPhoto()`.
- **Back stack:** Care is the start destination, so Plants may not be on the back stack. Never call `getBackStackEntry(Screen.PlantList.route)` unguarded (#836).
- **Tests:** `TodayQueueAggregatorWateredTodayTest` (list rules, order, rollover, horizon independence, `hasTasksDueNow`), a rollover case with an injected `MutableSharedFlow<LocalDate>` ticker in `TodayCareRepositoryTest`, and `WateredTodayLayoutTest` (placement index, pixel slot width, photo count) are JVM tests; the Compose cases are in `TodayScreenTest`, `TodayCareGridStickyHeaderTest` and `WateredTodayRowTest` (custom `Density`, row widths whose pixel remainder mod 6 is 4 or 5, asserting the "+N" circle and the sixth photo share the first photo's top). Many-group Compose tests use a viewport taller than the window (`tall = true` in `TodayScreenTest`) so lazy items compose without scrolling, then assert existence/order only. Sticky-header behaviour (pinning, hand-over, collapse + scroll) is tested in `TodayCareGridStickyHeaderTest` with a normal-height viewport and `performScrollToIndex`; the banner/pinned-header position case lives in `AwayModeBannerTest`.

# Fertilizing active seasons (#795, product ADR-0049; #813, product ADR-0051; #814, product ADR-0052)

**Fertilizing active seasons** (#795, product ADR-0049, superseding #286's four discrete per-season intervals, product ADR-0045) — one `fertilizingIntervalDays` (unchanged) plus a per-plant active-season set (`Plant.fertilizingSeasons: Set<FertilizingSeason>`, default all four; stored as comma-separated names in `PlantEntity.fertilizingSeasons: String?`, `null` = every season, `SeasonalFertilizing.encode()`/`decode()` is the one normalizer the repository and `BackupManager` both share). `CareSchedule.computeFertilizingDue()` computes the raw due date (`lastFertilizedAt + interval`, or the first-fertilize grace date) unchanged, then `SeasonalFertilizing.nextActiveDueAtMillis(rawDueAtMillis, activeSeasons, hemisphere, nowDate)` moves it out of an inactive season — a plant is therefore never due or overdue during an inactive season, and becomes due exactly on the first day of re-entry rather than "overdue by 3 months." A future raw date shifts by its own season; a past-or-present raw date is evaluated against *today's* season instead, because the raw date's own season can be active while an inactive stretch has since opened up between it and today — see `.claude/rules/schedule.md`'s Fertilizing bullet for the full branch-by-branch rule, including the contiguous-active-month-run lookup that correctly spans a year-boundary wrap (e.g. Autumn+Winter active). Same hemisphere derivation as seasonal watering (`SeasonalWatering.currentHemisphere()`). Add/Edit Plant and Plant Detail's Fertilize tab share one `FertilizingSeasonsSelector` chip row (`ui/components/`); the last selected chip can't be deselected, but as of #813 (product ADR-0051, amending product ADR-0049's disabled-chip and visible-label clauses) it stays enabled and selected rather than disabled — a tap on it wiggles, buzzes (`HapticFeedbackType.Reject`), and calls `onLastSeasonLocked` (never `onToggle`) so the screen shows a snackbar ("At least one season must stay active. To stop fertilizing, turn off the fertilizing reminder.") instead of silently doing nothing; repeated taps wiggle every time but the shared `SnackbarHostState.showSnackbarOnce()` helper (`ui/util/SnackbarExtensions.kt`) keeps the snackbar itself from queuing repeat copies. Selected chips carry a `primaryContainer` fill via the shared `yaptFilterChipColors()` (`ui/components/YaptFilterChipColors.kt`) that every `FilterChip` in the app now uses — a new `FilterChip` must pass `colors = yaptFilterChipColors()` (#814, product ADR-0052, amending product ADR-0051's season-chips-only scope; `DarkColorScheme` also now sets `secondaryContainer`/`onSecondaryContainer`); there is deliberately no checkmark `leadingIcon` (too busy on an all-selected row), and the outline an unselected chip has and a selected one drops is the non-color cue. The current season's chip shows a small decorative trailing dot instead of a `%1$s · Current season` label suffix; that wording now lives in the chip's `stateDescription` semantics for TalkBack. `FertilizingSeasonsSelector` reports a toggle of the tapped season, not a full replacement set (#804) — a full-set `onChange` let a second rapid tap silently discard the first, since the set was built from whatever `selected` snapshot that tap's composition still held. Add/Edit Plant applies the toggle to its own local form state (synchronous, can't race, no `PlantDetailViewModel.Event` equivalent needed); Plant Detail's `toggleFertilizingSeason()` (`PlantDetailScheduleSettingsActions.kt`) re-reads the plant fresh inside `plantEditMutex` and applies the toggle there, and its own empty-set rejection (the #804 race — two fast taps on the last two chips both passing the composable's own check) now emits `PlantDetailViewModel.Event.FertilizingSeasonToggleRejected` to show the same snackbar, without a wiggle, rather than silently doing nothing. No learning, confidence, or adjustment history — dormancy remains an independent, additional pause on top of this.
